Moutain Mist Tallow

Ingredients:

Beef Tallow, Coconut Oil, Jojoba Oil, Rosehip Oil, Vitamin E, Borage Oil, Tea Tree Oil, Neroli Oil, Lavender Oil, Ylang Ylang Oil, Helichrysum Oil

  • How to Apply: Scoop a small amount and warm between palms until melted. Massage into skin.
  • When to Use: Best applied at night, or anytime sun exposure isn’t immediate after use.
